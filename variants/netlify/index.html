Overview

How text-to-video and image-to-video work in Kling 4.0

Kling 4.0 offers two creative paths depending on what you start with — a text description or a reference image.

Text-to-video

Describe the scene, style, and cinematic mood you want. Kling 4.0 interprets your description and generates a stunning cinematic video that matches the brief. Best for quick ideation, social media content, and creative exploration.

Image-to-video

Upload a reference image and Kling 4.0 transforms it into a new cinematic video with your chosen style and settings. Best for product visuals, concept iteration, and building on existing designs.

FAQ

Questions about Kling 4.0 text-to-video and image-to-video

What is the difference between text-to-video and image-to-video in Kling 4.0?

Text-to-video generates a cinematic video from a text description. Image-to-video transforms an uploaded reference image into a new cinematic video with your chosen style.

Can I switch between text and image inputs?

Yes. Both workflows are available from the same interface. Choose text-to-video or image-to-video based on what you have.

Do I need video editing experience?

No. Kling 4.0 is designed for users without video editing experience. Describe or upload, choose settings, and generate.

What video formats can I export?

Kling 4.0 exports videos in standard formats playable on most devices and platforms.

Can I use Kling 4.0 videos commercially?

Commercial use depends on your plan. Paid plans include commercial licensing for generated videos.
